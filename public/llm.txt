## Technical reference

The watermark is applied by Google with standard alpha compositing:

  watermarked = α × logo + (1 − α) × original

where α is the per-pixel alpha of the 48×48 or 96×96 sparkle logo, logo is the RGB value of the sparkle sprite, and original is the RGB value produced by the generative model. Reverse alpha blending inverts this:

  original = (watermarked − α × 255) / (1 − α)

The 48×48 and 96×96 alpha maps are bundled with the page. Detection runs normalized cross-correlation (NCC) between each alpha map and the bottom-right corner of the image. Whichever map scores higher identifies the watermark size; the NCC peak position gives the corner offset.

For video, the pipeline demuxes the MP4 with mp4box.js, decodes a calibration frame at ~15% into the timeline to find the watermark position, then decodes every subsequent frame with the WebCodecs VideoDecoder, applies the reverse blend pixel-wise, re-encodes with VideoEncoder, and muxes the result with the original audio track bit-for-bit using mp4-muxer.

## What this tool does NOT do

- It does not remove SynthID, Google's invisible watermark baked into the pixel values of every Gemini image. SynthID detection requires Google's own verifier.
- It does not strip C2PA Content Credentials metadata. Most photo editors strip C2PA on re-save; SynthID survives.
- It does not remove watermarks from non-Google AI generators. Use a different tool for ChatGPT, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, etc.
